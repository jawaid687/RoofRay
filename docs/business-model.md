## 1. Market Opportunity & Tailwinds

The global rooftop solar PV market is projected to expand from \$110 Billion in 2024 to over \$280 Billion by 2032 (CAGR ~12.5%). Across South Asia, industrial transition policies, rising fossil-fuel electricity tariffs, and mandatory net-metering regulations (e.g. SREDA net metering in Bangladesh) are driving explosive commercial interest.

### The Customer Acquisition Bottleneck
In the solar EPC industry, **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** accounts for 15% to 22% of total project capital cost. Solar installers spend hundreds of dollars per prospective client conducting preliminary site visits, manual CAD drafting, and economic proposals, with closing rates below 18%.

RoofRay slashes pre-engineering turnaround from 2 weeks to **under 2 minutes**, fundamentally altering the economic funnel for solar EPCs and property owners.

## 2. Business Model Archetypes & Monetization Streams

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                     REVENUE ARCHITECTURE                        │
│                                                                 │
│  Stream 1: Solar EPC & Installer Pro SaaS (Subscription)        │

│  Stream 4: Enterprise White-Label & API Licensing               │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Stream 1: Solar EPC & Installer Pro SaaS (B2B Subscription)
- **Target:** Solar installers, EPC contractors, and electrical design consultants.
- **Value:** Unlimited 3D raycast shade simulations, multi-objective automated panel layout, exportable white-label client PDF reports, and AI Solar Advisor recommendations.
- **Pricing Tiers:**
  - *Starter EPC:* \$49/month (up to 30 projects/month, BDT & USD currency, PDF export).
  - *Growth EPC:* \$149/month (unlimited projects, custom branding, high-density panel placement).
  - *Enterprise:* \$399/month (API access, multi-user seats, team workflow management).

### Stream 2: Commercial & Industrial (C&I) Turnkey Assessment
- **Target:** Factory owners (Ready-Made Garments, textile mills, pharmaceuticals, cold storages).
- **Value:** Deep-dive multi-building portfolio analysis, SREDA 70% sanctioned load compliance verification, and institutional-grade financial cash-flow models for green financing (IDCOL / commercial bank loans).
- **Pricing:** \$300 to \$800 per audited factory complex.

### Stream 3: Qualified Solar Lead Marketplace (B2B Lead Generation)
- **Target:** Residential and commercial building owners using the public RoofRay platform who request installation quotes.
- **Value:** Instead of low-intent raw contact lists, RoofRay provides EPCs with high-intent, 3D pre-simulated solar projects with known roof geometry, shading profile, capacity, and estimated capex.
- **Pricing:** 2% to 3% referral commission upon project closing (typically \$500 to \$2,500 per closed commercial project).

## 4. Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

1. **Strategic Partnerships with Renewable Energy Associations:** Partner with SREDA-registered solar EPC contractors in Bangladesh and regional solar associations in South/Southeast Asia.
2. **Bottom-Up CleanTech Freemium:** Free public access for building owners on `/` and `/app` with essential 3D simulation and clear-sky raycasting to build organic domain authority.
3. **Green Banking Integration:** Present RoofRay reports to non-bank financial institutions (e.g. IDCOL in Bangladesh, climate investment funds) as standardized feasibility documentation for subsidized renewable energy credit lines.

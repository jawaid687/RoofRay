"Honorable judges and fellow innovators. Across global cities and rapidly industrializing hubs like Dhaka, millions of square meters of rooftop real estate remain untapped. Property owners want clean solar power to beat skyrocketing electricity bills. 

Yet today, rooftop solar planning is plagued by manual friction. Conducting a preliminary site survey takes two weeks, costs over a thousand dollars, and frequently fails because of **urban shading**. When a neighboring high-rise or rooftop water tank casts an unmodeled shadow, the internal physics of solar strings cause power generation to plummet by 30% to 50%. Solar installers lose deals, and property owners lose money."
